# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help.

## What is expected

- Keep criticism about the code and the evidence, not the person.
- Say what you actually verified and what you did not. "I could not test
  this, I have no such device" is a welcome sentence here; a claim that
  turns out to be untested is not.
- Accept that a maintainer with one keyboard cannot verify your hardware,
  and that a contributor with different hardware cannot verify theirs
  against a device they lack. Say so plainly and move on.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning language. Sustained pressure on a
volunteer maintainer's time. Deliberately misrepresenting what you tested.

## Enforcement

Report concerns by opening an issue, or privately to the maintainer via the
email on their GitHub profile. The maintainer may edit, hide, or delete
comments, and may block accounts, when this document is not being followed.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
kept short deliberately: a document nobody reads protects nobody.
